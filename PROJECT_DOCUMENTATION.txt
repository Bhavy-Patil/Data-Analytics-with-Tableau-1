================================================================================
GLOBAL ELECTRIC MOBILITY INTELLIGENCE & ADOPTION STUDY - PROJECT DOSSIER
Focus: Macroeconomic Powertrain Trends, OEM Market Share & Battery Transition
================================================================================

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & STRATEGIC CONTEXT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This project delivers a comprehensive intelligence platform analyzing 200,000 
commercial and passenger electric vehicle data points across worldwide assembly 
hubs and consumer markets. The analytics pipeline evaluates market penetration, 
battery pack chemistry viability, range-to-capacity efficiency ratios, and 
lifecycle carbon emissions displacement.

Target Stakeholder Personas:
- Chief Strategy Officer (Automotive OEM): Portfolio white-space identification
  and segment pricing strategies.
- Mobility Fleet Director: Total cost of ownership, mean autonomy per charge,
  and consumer reliability satisfaction scores.
- Clean Energy Policy Advisor: Territorial manufacturing capacity, battery raw
  material transitions, and gross carbon offsets.

2. DATA ARCHITECTURE & CORE PIPELINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Observation Volume: 200,000 validated records
- Dimensional Fields: 39 analytical attributes (technological, financial, geographic)
- Relational Schema: Ingested via PostgreSQL/MySQL `fact_ev_market_records`
  with composite B-Tree indexing on vehicle segment, OEM brand, and build year.

3. TAILORED TABLEAU CALCULATED FIELDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Calc 1 - Deliveries in Millions:
    [Annual Sales Units] / 1000000

* Calc 2 - High Autonomy Index:
    IF [Driving Range Km] >= 350 AND [Battery Capacity Kwh] <= 85 
    THEN "Optimal Efficiency Tier" 
    ELSE "Standard Tier" 
    END

* Calc 3 - Market Segment Classifier:
    IF [Price USD] < 30000 THEN "Entry Mobility"
    ELSEIF [Price USD] <= 60000 THEN "Mid-Tier Commercial/Family"
    ELSEIF [Price USD] <= 100000 THEN "Premium Performance"
    ELSE "Ultra-Luxury / Specialized"
    END

* Calc 4 - Specific Battery Productivity (km/kWh):
    AVG([Driving Range Km]) / AVG([Battery Capacity Kwh])

* Calc 5 - Commercial Sales Notation:
    "$" + STR(ROUND(SUM([Annual Sales Units]) / 1000000, 1)) + "M Delivered"
